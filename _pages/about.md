Hello! I'm a postdoctoral research associate at the University of Colorado
Boulder, working on quantum gravity and quantum information.

Most of my research uses the gravitational path integral to ask what quantum
mechanics looks like when spacetime itself is dynamical. A question I keep
returning to is why entropy in gravity is computed by geometry — the
Ryu-Takayanagi/HRT formula equates an entanglement entropy with the area of a
surface — and what Hilbert space and algebra of observables that entropy
actually belongs to. Along the way this has led me to real-time (Lorentzian)
replica wormholes and their complex saddle geometries, to von Neumann algebras
constructed directly from the path integral, to the factorization puzzles raised
by Euclidean wormholes, and most recently to approximate quantum error
correction in topologically ordered phases.

There is an overview of these directions on the [research](/research/) page, and
a full list of papers, with abstracts, on the [publications](/publications/)
page.

Before Boulder I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Illinois
Urbana-Champaign. I received my Ph.D. from the University of California, Santa
Barbara in 2023 and my B.S. from Nankai University in 2018.

I'm always glad to hear from people interested in these questions — feel free to
reach me at <zhencheng.wang@colorado.edu>.
